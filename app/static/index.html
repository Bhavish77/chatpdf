Built to show its work

Every answer is traceable back to the exact source, and the pipeline tells you how it got there.

Self-correcting retrieval

The chat graph grades its own retrieved passages, rewrites the query once if they fall short, and checks its final answer for groundedness — visible in a step-by-step panel.

Real citations

Every claim links to the document and page it came from. Click a citation to see the source snippet and jump straight to that page.

Async, crash-safe ingestion

Upload and keep working. A background queue parses, chunks, and embeds your documents, and survives a worker restart without duplicating work.

Private by account

Every document, chat, and quota is scoped to your account. Delete everything, permanently, whenever you want.

Bring your own documents

PDF, DOCX, TXT, or Markdown. Tag one or several with @filename, or search everything you've uploaded at once.

Nothing to install

Runs entirely on free-tier infrastructure — Gemini's free API, a free Postgres, a free web host. Open source and self-hostable.

How it works

Upload

Drag in a PDF, DOCX, TXT, or Markdown file. Watch it move from queued to ready in real time.

Tag

Type @ to scope a question to one or more documents, or leave it untagged to search everything.

Ask

Get a streamed, cited answer — and a transparent trace of how the system found it.
